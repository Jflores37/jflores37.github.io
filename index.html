About

I manage a T-Mobile retail store, and I taught myself to build the data tools I wished I had on the floor. It started by rebuilding a broken store-performance workbook — fixing 403 formula errors and making the totals actually total. After I rolled it out, my store moved into the top 25% of the district.

That turned into a habit. Since then I've shipped live market dashboards backed by real data pipelines, a momentum-scanner terminal, options-analytics scanners, and a handful of automation bots — building, breaking, and fixing in public on nights and weekends.

Now I'm moving into data analytics full-time. The instinct is the same one that moved my store's numbers: take messy data and turn it into something a person can act on in ten seconds. That's the whole job, and it's the part I like most.
